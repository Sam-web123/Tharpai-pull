What is Tharpai Pull?

Tharpai Pull (also known as Darbha or Kusha grass — Desmostachya bipinnata) is one of the most sacred grasses in Hindu tradition. Revered since Vedic times, it is an indispensable part of religious ceremonies including Tarpan, Shradh, Homa, and daily Puja. Its long, slender blades are believed to carry powerful purifying energy, connecting the devotee to the divine and to their ancestors.


Priests and devotees use Tharpai Pull to fashion sacred rings (pavitri), as a seat for idols, as an offering, and as a purifying mat during rituals. According to Ayurveda, the grass also holds cooling and detoxifying properties that promote mental clarity and spiritual well-being.
